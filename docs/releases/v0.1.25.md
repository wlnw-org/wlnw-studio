## What's new

- **Camera-trap sequences in exports.** Photos from one camera taken a minute or less apart are now one sequence, and all sightings from one video are one sequence. The WDX export marks each record with its sequence (as a group), and the Camtrap DP export fills in eventID, so tools that read either file can count a burst of photos as one visit instead of several.

## Fixed

- The WDX export now reports how many records it actually wrote. It used to include sound detections it left out because they had no time or place.
