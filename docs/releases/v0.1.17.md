## What's new

- **Clips from a Wildlife Network Base play in Review.** A Base saves its clips as AAC (.m4a files), which Studio could not open before. Review now plays them, lets you move through them and shows their spectrogram, on Windows, Mac and Linux, with nothing extra to install. AAC clips from any BirdNET-Go station work the same way.
- **Opus clips.** Studio still cannot play clips that BirdNET-Go saves as Opus. If your station uses Opus, choose WAV, FLAC or AAC in BirdNET-Go's audio export settings; clips saved after that can be reviewed in Studio.
