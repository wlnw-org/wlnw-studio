## What's new

- **Agreement figures for papers**: when two people review the same clips, the comparison table now shows a 95% interval next to every agreement percentage and kappa, plus precision per species (of the clips reviewed at or above the threshold, the share confirmed).
- **Save table for a paper (CSV)**: one row per species and an "All species" row, ready for a supplement.
- **Methods sentence**: a ready-to-paste paragraph describing how the review was done, filled in with your project's numbers.
