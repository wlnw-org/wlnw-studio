## What's new

- **Camera-trap videos.** Add MP4, MOV and AVI files on the Recordings screen, next to your sound files and photos. Studio needs FFmpeg to read video: if it is not on your computer, the Recordings screen offers a one-time download (about 30 MB on Mac, 120 MB on Windows), checked before it is used.
- **The sound of a video is listened to.** A video's sound track goes through the same bird and bat models as your recordings, and detection times count from the start of the video.
- **One animal, one sighting.** Studio looks at the video one frame a second (you can change this on the Process screen, from one frame every ten seconds to ten a second) and follows each animal from frame to frame, so a fox walking past for twenty seconds is one sighting, not twenty.
- **Sightings come with a picture and a clip.** For each sighting Studio keeps the sharpest frame and cuts a short clip. Both show on the Images screen, where you review them like photos. They are saved in a folder next to your project.
- **Videos in your exports.** CSV, WDX, Darwin Core and Camtrap DP exports give the video's file name and the start and end of each sighting.
