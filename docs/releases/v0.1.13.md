## What's new

- **Credits and licences.** A new Credits and licences window, linked at the bottom of every screen, lists each model Studio uses, who made it, its licence and the paper to cite. The same credit now shows next to each model on the Find species screen.
- **Citations in your exports.** Exports now name the paper behind each model. The CSV has a new last column called model_reference. The Darwin Core Archive for GBIF fills in identificationReferences and lists the citations in its description. WDX and Camtrap DP carry them too. Raven, Audacity and Kaleidoscope files have no room for this, so a short CITATION.txt is saved next to them.
- **Bat results are named in full.** Exports now say BatDetect2 UK instead of batdetect2-uk.

## Licences

- **The BatDetect2 part of Studio is now CC BY-NC 4.0.** Studio's bat processing is a direct translation of BatDetect2's code, so at the request of its authors it now has BatDetect2's own licence and its own folder in the source code. It is for non-commercial use only. The rest of Studio stays Apache 2.0.
- **BirdNET.** The BirdNET team has given permission to include BirdNET 2.4 in Studio for research and conservation. Its own licence, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, stays with the model files.

## The bat model

- Wildlife Network does not host the BatDetect2 model, at the request of its authors, who publish it themselves. As before, you make the model files from the official BatDetect2 package and add them on the Find species screen. The steps are in the bat guide at wlnw.org/help.
- If you already added the bat files, nothing changes.
