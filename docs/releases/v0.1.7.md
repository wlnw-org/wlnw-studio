## What's new

- **Share your checked answers (optional, off unless you turn it on)**: in Settings, Studio can share your yes and no answers to help build free regional classifiers for places that BirdNET and Perch know poorly. It sends the species, your answer, the clip's Perch embedding (numbers that describe the sound, not audio), the place rounded to about 100 km, and the year and week. Never audio, file names, exact times or anything about you. "Delete everything I shared" removes it all from wlnw.org at once. See https://wlnw.org/help/studio/share-answers
