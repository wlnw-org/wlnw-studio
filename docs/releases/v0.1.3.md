## What's new

- **Camera traps**: Studio finds camera-trap photos next to your recordings and identifies animals with SpeciesNet (with MegaDetector v6), filtered by each camera's country. Review photos in a grid with the boxes drawn, and export to Camtrap DP, Darwin Core or WDX. Photos with people in them are never exported or published.
- **Bats**: BatDetect2 for UK bats on ultrasonic recordings (192 kHz and up), with clips played 10 times slower so calls can be heard. Non-commercial use only; add the model from a folder.
- **Import results from other tools**: BirdNET-Analyzer, BirdNET-Pi and BirdNET-Go, Raven tables and Kaleidoscope, reviewed and published without reprocessing. Kaleidoscope export too.
- **Second reviewer**: two people review separately, compare agreement per species (Cohen's kappa) and settle the differences.
- **Wildlife Network when online**: Review flags species not recorded within 25 km of the recorder, shows usual arrival dates, photos and nearby protected areas. Only the recorder's position rounded to about 10 km and species names are sent; it can be turned off.
- **Linux**: AppImage and deb downloads (Ubuntu 24.04, Debian 13, Fedora 40 or newer).
- **Automatic updates**: Studio checks for a new version at startup and can install it and restart.
