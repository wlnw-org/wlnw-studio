## What's new

- **Occupancy tables**: Export and publish has Occupancy by day and Occupancy by week. One row per species and site, one column per day or week: 1 detected, 0 recorded but not detected, NA not recorded. A second file gives the minutes recorded per site, ready for occupancy models in R (unmarked, spOccupancy).
- **Find more, smarter**: after Find more like this, mark the clips and choose Find more. The next search uses every clip you marked as that species and skips the ones you already marked, so each round brings new and closer matches.
