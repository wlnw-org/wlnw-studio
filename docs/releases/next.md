## What's new

- **See what your reviewing does.** The Review screen now has a Your reviewing card: how many answers you have given (today, and how many days in a row), how many species have their own threshold out of those with enough detections to get one, and what your answers changed: how many detections the default threshold would have kept that are now left out (most of them likely wrong), and how many it would have dropped that are now kept.
- **A clear goal for each species.** Until a species has its own threshold, the list shows how far along it is, for example 5 of 8 checked. Only yes and no answers count toward the 8; unsure does not.
- **The answer that sets a threshold says so.** When an answer gives a species its own threshold, Studio tells you how many detections it now keeps and how often they are estimated to be right.
