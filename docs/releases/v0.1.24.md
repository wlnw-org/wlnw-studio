## What's new

- **FFmpeg download for every computer.** Intel Macs and Linux (64-bit) can now download FFmpeg from the Recordings screen too, so camera-trap videos work without installing anything yourself. Each download is checked against a fixed checksum before it is used. On Linux it needs Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12 or newer.
- **Short clips for long sightings.** When an animal stays in view for most of a video, its clip is now at most 10 seconds around the sharpest frame instead of the whole video. You can choose 5 to 60 seconds on the Process screen. Exports still give the full time the animal was in view.
- **Country for video cameras.** A camera that has only videos now gets a Country box on the Recordings screen, like cameras with photos, so SpeciesNet can rule out species that do not live there.
- **Better suggestions.** The other species suggested for a photo or video sighting now leave out species that do not live in the camera's country, as the main answer already did. Run the camera again (or change its country) to update earlier results.

## Fixed

- Videos filmed on a phone held upright are now listed with their size as filmed (portrait). Their pictures and clips were already the right way up; tests now check this for iPhone-style HEVC videos.
