BirdNET V2.4, as packaged for Wildlife Network Studio

BirdNET is by the K. Lisa Yang Center for Conservation Bioacoustics at the Cornell Lab of Ornithology and Chemnitz
University of Technology. https://github.com/birdnet-team/BirdNET-Analyzer

These files are BirdNET V2.4 converted to the ONNX format Studio loads (the network and the location model), with
the spectrogram constants and species labels taken from the official model. They are offered with the permission
of the BirdNET team (Stefan Kahl, email of 11 November 2026) for research and conservation.
